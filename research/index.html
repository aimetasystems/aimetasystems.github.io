What is a Metasurface?

A metasurface is an ultrathin optical device composed of arrays of subwavelength structures called meta-atoms.

By carefully designing each meta-atom, we can control the phase, amplitude, and polarization of light.

AI-Driven Metasurface Design

We use artificial intelligence to discover meta-atom geometries and metasurface designs for desired optical responses. Our goal is to accelerate inverse design and explore complex structures that are difficult to design manually.
