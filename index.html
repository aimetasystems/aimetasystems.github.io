A.I. integrated Metasystems Lab

Lab Overview

AIM Lab explores intelligent metasystems that combine artificial intelligence, electromagnetic modeling, and metasurface engineering to control light in new ways.

We develop computational approaches for designing and optimizing advanced optical systems for wavefront control, sensing, imaging, and other photonic applications.
